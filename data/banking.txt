
BANKING SERVICES KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. ACCOUNT TYPES
- Savings Account: Good for saving money. Earns 2.5% annual interest. No monthly fee if balance > $500.
- Checking Account: Good for daily spending. Includes debit card. $10 monthly fee (waived with direct deposit).
- Student Account: No monthly fees for students under 25.
- Business Account: For small business owners. Includes payroll tools.

2. CARDS
- Debit Card: Linked to checking account. Withdraw cash at ATMs.
- Credit Card: Borrow money up to a limit. Pay back later. 
  - Gold Card: 2% cash back on all purchases. $100 annual fee.
  - Basic Card: No annual fee. 1% cash back on groceries.

3. LOANS
- Personal Loan: Up to $50,000. Interest rates start at 7%.
- Mortgage: For buying a home. 15 or 30 year terms.
- Auto Loan: For buying a car. Rates start at 4%.

4. DIGITAL BANKING
- Mobile App: Check balance, transfer money, pay bills.
- Online Banking: Login at www.ourbank.com.
- Zelle: Send money to friends instantly.

5. SECURITY
- Never share your password or PIN.
- We will never ask for your password via email or phone.
- Enable 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) for extra safety.
- Report lost cards immediately at 1-800-BANK-HELP.

6. FEES
- Overdraft Fee: $35 if you spend more than you have.
- ATM Fee: Free at our ATMs. $3 at other ATMs.
- Wire Transfer: $25 domestic, $45 international.
